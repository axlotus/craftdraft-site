CraftDraft

Turn your handmade listings into scheduled pins, one approved design at a time.

CraftDraft is a personal tool in development. It turns the owner's own Etsy listing photos into pin designs. Every pin is reviewed, edited and approved by hand before it is scheduled and published to the owner's own boards. Nothing is posted automatically.

CraftDraft is not available to the public yet.
