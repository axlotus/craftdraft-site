Privacy policy

CraftDraft. Last updated 30 October 2026.

Who we are

CraftDraft is a personal tool, currently used only by its owner for his own Etsy shop and his own Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public.

What data CraftDraft uses

  • Etsy: listing titles, descriptions, tags, sections and photos from the owner's own shop, read with the owner's permission, to design pins.
  • Pinterest: the owner's own boards (to choose where a pin goes) and the pins CraftDraft creates. Access is granted through Pinterest's own sign in screen (OAuth). CraftDraft never asks for or stores a Pinterest or Etsy password.
  • Access tokens: the sign in tokens Pinterest and Etsy issue, kept only to act on the owner's behalf.

How data is stored

Data stays on the owner's own computer. Information from Pinterest is fetched when needed and is not kept, apart from the identifiers of pins CraftDraft created and their publishing receipts. Tokens are stored privately and never shared.

What CraftDraft does not do

  • It does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.
  • It does not use data for advertising, profiling or training artificial intelligence models.
  • It does not read other people's pins, boards or shops.
  • It does not post anything without the owner choosing and approving each pin.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings (Security, Apps) and on Etsy at etsy.com/your/apps. After access is revoked or deletion is requested, all related data is deleted within 30 days.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Changes

If CraftDraft is ever offered to other people, this policy will be updated before that happens, and the date above will change.
